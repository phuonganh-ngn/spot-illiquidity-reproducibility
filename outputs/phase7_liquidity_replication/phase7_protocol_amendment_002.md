# Phase-7 protocol amendment 002: placebo and classification rules

This pre-external amendment was written before the corrected development-selection rerun completed and without inspecting external outcomes or forecast scores. It clarifies the fixed displacement placebo and makes the paper-level decision rule operational. It does not change assets, dates, targets, feature sets, estimators, model families, development folds, transformation candidates, primary scoring, bootstrap settings or the single external evaluation.

For the derivative-information displacement placebo, use a fixed forward offset of 42 four-hour intervals (seven days), with no circular wraparound. Drop observations whose shifted feature history would cross the available panel boundary. A circular wrap would connect unrelated dates at sample boundaries and would create a synthetic temporal adjacency; avoiding that is the reason for this clarification. The separate post-outcome placebo uses derivative history t+7 through t+12, entirely after the target window t+1 through t+6. Both tests are diagnostic only and cannot select the primary model.

For each asset/outcome/information-set comparison, compute the date-level MAE-loss difference for each learned model, average MLP and GRU within the single neural category, and then give each of the five categories equal weight. The primary Amihud external-replication criterion requires: (a) the equal-category mean loss difference is below zero for BTC and ETH; (b) its paired 42-interval bootstrap 95% interval has an upper endpoint below zero for both BTC and ETH; (c) at least four of five eligible assets have a negative equal-category mean; (d) at least three of five categories improve in both BTC and ETH; and (e) at least two of the three fixed external calendar segments improve in both BTC and ETH, with no segment showing deterioration exceeding 5% of that segment's Spot-only MAE. All five conditions are required.

Cross-measure generalization requires at least one of the two distinct liquidity proxies (Kyle price impact or Corwin-Schultz range spread) to satisfy conditions (a), (b) and (d) for both BTC and ETH. If Amihud passes but neither distinct proxy passes, the finding is Amihud-specific predictive value. If the sign or bootstrap criteria fail, classify the external replication as not established or model/asset sensitive as appropriate. No threshold is relaxed after external scoring.
